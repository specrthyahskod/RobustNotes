Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This policy describes how RobustNotes handles information in the RobustNotes Android app and the RobustNotes desktop app. RobustNotes is developed by the RobustNotes project. Contact support@robustnotes.com with privacy questions.

Android app

The Android app stores the notes you create in a private file in the app's internal storage on your device. The app does not require an account, does not send note content to RobustNotes or another server, does not include advertising or analytics, and does not request internet or broad file-access permissions. Android automatic backup is disabled for the app.

You can edit or delete notes in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its private app data. The app does not currently provide cloud sync, cross-device transfer, or a separate export feature.

Desktop app

The desktop app stores profiles and documents on the computer. Optional handwriting recognition runs locally after its model is installed. The optional AI chat sends the question and selected note or page context to the model endpoint configured by the user; that endpoint may be local or remote. Review the endpoint and its provider's privacy terms before sending private content. The desktop app can create password-encrypted backup archives at a location selected by the user.

Information collection and sharing

The Android app does not collect or share personal data with RobustNotes. Notes may contain personal information if you choose to write it, but the app keeps that content in its private on-device storage. RobustNotes does not receive it. The desktop app's optional external AI endpoint is the exception described above.

Security and retention

Android stores notes in app-private storage managed by the operating system; app-level note encryption is not currently provided. Notes remain on the device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Keep your device protected with its screen lock and avoid sharing a device profile with people you do not trust.

Children

RobustNotes is a general productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children. The Android app does not knowingly collect children's personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page when the app's data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be reflected here before a release that introduces them.
